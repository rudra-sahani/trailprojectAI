# Privacy & Peer-Quote Redaction Policy

VeriReview AI enforces strict privacy and anonymization rules to protect peer feedback providers and prevent retaliation.

## Redaction Rules
1. **Peer Feedback Anonymity:** Raw verbatim peer quotes are redacted and aggregated in all employee-facing views and general reports.
2. **Reviewer Session Exception:** Only the assigned manager during an active review session or HR Admin in the Audit Center can view source attribution metadata where permitted.
3. **API-Level Enforcement:** Redaction occurs at the API service and database view layer, ensuring unredacted peer quotes are never transmitted over the wire to unauthorized clients.
